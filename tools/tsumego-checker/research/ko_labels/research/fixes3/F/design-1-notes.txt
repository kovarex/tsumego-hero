1. What the stalled searches spend their nodes on

Base runs of the five leaves (runs/k0xx-base.log): every undecided part is a counted search (solvex) or the bent-four
solve that gets 1,000,000 nodes, the engine's fresh retry another 1,000,000, the 5,000,000 escalation and its retry, and
a one-ply solvemoves at 2,000,000 per child, and stays undecided. Many other searches are decided only by the retry,
in a few thousand nodes after 1,000,000 wasted (k023 "W 1 ... 0 4 pw=1" 1,003,298; k041 "W ... 1 0" 1,015,924; k035's
solvemoves children G19, H17, J18 about 1,005,000 each).

Replayed through the instrumented engine (stats/engine_o0, fresh history, 1,000,000 nodes):
- k043 count s=1 (solvex B 0 ... 1 0): 1,000,001 nodes over 3,791 positions; one position (path 0 2 19 40 19 38 20 -1
  4 5 24 114 115 -1 39, depth 16) was expanded 994,774 times, and each of its 994,774 stores was refused. The other
  3,790 positions took 5,227 nodes together.
- k023 count s=3: one position 986,135 times (depth 16), 9,183 positions in all.
- k035 count s=1: one position 988,746 times (depth 7), 9,012 positions.
- k041 count s=0: one position 997,980 times (depth 9), 1,563 positions.
Not the repetitions (20 to 296 adjudicated), not the threat bookkeeping (the looping node has the root's threats), not
the fringe, not the move order as such: a loop in the proof search.

Cause (engine.cpp, df-pn): store() keeps a proven entry of another position in a slot rather than a position's unproven
numbers. The slot index is the key's low 22 bits, so with a few thousand positions a collision is likely. A position X
whose slot holds a proof has no entry, so lookup() gives (1, 1). Its parent has just searched X; the next aggregate sees
X as a new child with pn = dn = 1, picks it again, calls mid(X) with a threshold X's real numbers already exceed, mid
returns at once, the store is refused again: one node per round, until the budget runs out. The fresh retry has another
move order, so the positions often land in other slots: that is why a search undecided at 1,000,000 nodes so often
took a few thousand in the retry (the "endless cycling" the retry was added for).

2. The fix (engine.cpp)

mid() hands its node's last numbers to the parent (found), the parent keeps them in the child (ProofChild pn, dn,
searched), and childValues() uses them when the table has no entry for a child searched in this expansion. A search
whose table loses no entry runs exactly as before. The same four searches, fresh, through stats/engine_fix_o0: k043
18,729 nodes (N, the research's value), k023 17,001 (K), k035 42,652 (N), k041 32,496 (N); 2 to 159 stores are still
refused, without a loop.

4. The time limit inside a search (engine set deadline, Solver.ask)

With the loop gone a search that really stays undecided spends its budget on new positions, and those cost far more than
the loop's (generate() and the end tests of every child of a new position; the loop's one position had them cached). In
the first regression run with the fix alone (after-fix1.jsonl, stopped at 56 leaves) 9139 "D17 ... A15" timed out at
669.7 s (19.1M nodes; the base at 323.8 s, 74.8M nodes) and 10489's first leaf at 726.1 s (base 313.3 s): the limit was
read only between searches, and one escalated count search (5,000,000 nodes and its retry) ran for minutes at about
30,000 nodes a second (9139's class solve: 4,000,004 nodes in 132 s). The engine now gives up at the problem's time
limit (set deadline, read every 4,096 nodes), and Solver.ask raises TimeoutError for a search answered undecided after
it, so nothing the limit cut is kept as undecided. 9139 now times out at 300.2 s. The five cases and the first 56 leaves
of the regression give the same labels with and without it (a leaf that ends before its limit runs exactly as before).

the BENT4-open labels of 2154, 556, 9957, 10005, 10051, 13547 k042 and 11263's DSKO/KO, the "?" of 11280, 13410, 13489
two and 13547, 15293 KO?, k023 A-?KO?, k043 KO?, k041 A?KO?, k035 A-1KO?). So these changes are the fix's, not the

The cause is the same loop wherever it was looked at (stats/replay_all.out, the twelve stalled or retried searches of
the five cases, and 9925's seki-in-sente test): the unchanged engine, fresh, spends 979,181 to 997,980 of 1,000,000 nodes
on one position whose store is refused every time, 10 of 12 searches; the other two (k023 "W 1 .. pw=1", k041 "W .. 1 0")
were the ones the base decided only in its retry, and fresh they take 3,297 and 15,923 nodes in both engines (no store
refused). The fixed engine decides all of them in 3,297 to 57,680 nodes, 9925's in 54,513 (K: SEKI+).

7. Risks and open points

- The fix changes nothing in a search whose table loses no entry. Elsewhere the search goes on instead of looping, so
  its move-order history (never cleared by a retry now) and its proof table differ from the baseline's afterwards. The
  regression shows no decided label lost or changed except the five above; GHI-sensitive searches (k049's count) can
  still come out either way with the order of the searches before them.
- The proof table still stores verdicts that rest on a repetition of a position above the node (design section 2, the
  GHI problem). k049's count is one such search; the research's narrow count 2 for k049 rests on it (section 3).
  variants/ghi shows the cure (not storing such a verdict, handing it to the parent with the path index it rests on): the
  same five labels, k049's count 1 under every cycle rule. It changes which verdicts every repetition-heavy search can
  reuse, so it needs its own regression run before it can go in.
- Cost: a search that stays undecided now spends its budget on new positions (about 30,000 nodes a second in the
  slowest areas) where the loop failed in seconds. Leaves whose class stays undecided take up to the limit: four 13489
  leaves went from "?" in 5 to 11 s to a time-out at 300 s or A?KO? in 113 s. The deadline keeps every leaf at the limit
  (max 300.9 s; before 438 s); the run is still half as long as the baseline's.
- The deadline makes a search answered undecided after the limit a time-out; a leaf whose last search used to finish
  after its limit (9942 at 300.3 s in the run without the deadline) times out now.
- k023 and k043 (and the owner's 1b for k023) need a rule for a capture in a second ko, not a search fix; design-2's
  second-ko readings are the place.
- 13 newly decided labels differ from S[] without a research verdict (section 5); they are what the checker computes
  once its searches finish, and some carry wall warnings.
