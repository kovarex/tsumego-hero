## 3. The switching reading can open on a second-ko capture whose outright test is undecided, and still print a decided label (undecided-or-timeout; tools/tsumego-checker/tsumego.py line 1621)

Scenario: Take a profile with n_X >= 2 where the candidate table at the first approach position (solvemoves at s=0, o=Lo, Y forced to pass n times) leaves the second-ko capture at U. profile() keeps undecided candidates (line 1376 tests 'wins_move is not False'), and line 1621 builds `seconds` without looking at wins. The reading's premise is that the capture is one of X's free moves that keep the outright win. If that is undecided, the choice between A-(n-1)KO and A-nKO is undecided too. counted_moves (1079-1097) never records an undecided search, so the label text does not even list one.

Evidence: 10403 "B18 A18 C19 A16 A19", with only the candidate table's verdict for A17 patched to U (scripts/undecided_gate.py; every other search is real): "PATCHED candidate table [... ('A17', None) ...]", "LABEL A-1KO- | approaches {S: 1, O: 0} | switching A17". The text names A17 as the switching capture and has no 'Undecided:' part.

Proposed fix: Build `seconds` only from candidates with wins True. If the only second-ko captures among the candidates are undecided and the (n-1)-form counts d_full, set the unsettled 'switching' bracket (n-1 or n) instead of switching.
