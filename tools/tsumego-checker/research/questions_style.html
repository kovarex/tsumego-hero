Reading the diagrams

Numbers continue the move count from the start of the problem, so the tree's own moves keep their numbers. Plain stones were on the board before the numbered moves. Faded stones were captured within the diagram, with their number if they had one. A square marks a ko point that may not be retaken at once. Letters mark points the text names, and a dashed outline marks a search frame given by hand.

The note after a caption lists what the board cannot show: a move elsewhere, which keeps its number; a retake right after a ko threat elsewhere, whose threat and answer are neither drawn nor numbered; captures; and a stone played where a numbered stone was captured.

Reading the solver lines

Each line marked Solver checks the position after the diagram's last move, with the side to move there. All {{CHECKS}} checks ran when this page was made, on {{DATE}}; a check that failed, or stayed undecided at {{BUDGET}} nodes, would have stopped the page.

Threats are ko threats elsewhere on the board; a retake against the ko ban uses one. Local threats are moves in the position, and the solver reads them. "White must pass its first turn" means White plays elsewhere at its next turn, which gives Black one free move: this is how approach moves are counted. "Black wins when it has one threat more than White, and fails with an equal number" sums up two searches, each with the fewest threats that give that difference.

The solver reads only its search area: by default the checker's area for the problem; the wider area adds two rings of empty points; a search frame is given by hand. Outside the area the board counts as the attacker's wall; the default and wider areas still count the empty points just outside them as liberties, and a search frame does not.

In the default area, a status line that finds a ko asks the wider area again, as the checker does. With the end playout, two passes do not end the game while a defender chain near the target is in atari: play goes on, and the attacker must take it to win. Seki counts as life.
