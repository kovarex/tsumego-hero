Changed: Each 200-problem part of a collection now has its own best accuracy and best time, taken from your attempts since your last reset of that part, mistakes on problems you went on to solve included. The bests you had were wrong, often 100% though you made mistakes, so they were all reset: finish a part again to set its best. The accuracy and speed achievements of collections and Superior Accuracy were reset with them and are earned again per part of at least 100 problems; you keep the XP they gave you, and earning one again gives its XP as usual. The set page shows the bests at collection size 200, where a page is one part, and a collection card gets its 100% frame when every part it shows has a best of 100%.
