Added: A daily streak flame in the menu counts the days in a row on which you earned the XP of 3 problems of your rank (mistake training XP included) and solved 3 mistake training reviews, or as many as were due. The flame flares up the moment you complete the day, and hovering it shows today's XP as a bar and your reviews. Time mode doesn't count, since it gives no XP. Your day ends at 3 AM your time, and every 7 days in a row earn a freeze that saves the streak on a day you miss.
Removed: The mistake training no longer counts its own streak of cleared days; its calendar stays on your profile.
